# Aangan Habitat | City, Measurement and Pre-Visit Guide
Document owner: Project Operations | Version: 1.0 | 4 September 2025

## Coverage and site visits
The active residential service cluster is Pune, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane, and Nashik. A site visit is scheduled only after a brief qualification call and confirmation that the building permits the proposed work. Visits outside this cluster require an operations review; the chatbot should not promise travel, a fee waiver, or a start date. We do not undertake overseas execution.

## Measurements used for estimates
Interior package rates are applied to carpet area for an early illustration. Carpet area is the usable internal floor area, excluding external walls and common areas; the final measurement is checked at site. A builder's saleable or built-up area is not interchangeable with carpet area. For renovation, the final price depends on measured scope and existing conditions, so the package rate is only a first-pass comparison.

### What to have ready for a consultation
| Item | Why the team asks | If unavailable |
|---|---|---|
| City and locality | Confirms service capacity and travel | Share the nearest city |
| Project type and room count | Selects the right team and scope | A short description is enough |
| Carpet area or approximate dimensions | Supports an indicative estimate | Use a rough range and label it approximate |
| Possession / desired start window | Checks sequence and site readiness | Record the preferred month |
| Budget range | Aligns finish and scope | “Not decided” is an acceptable answer |
| Name plus phone or email | Enables follow-up with consent | Ask one contact method, not both |

## Building and society constraints
Before mobilization the customer or building representative should clarify work hours, lift protection, debris movement, water/electricity availability, shut-down windows, and any hot-work permit. Society rules and permissions are controlled by the building; Aangan can provide a work plan but does not guarantee approval. Hidden wiring, leakage, or substrate damage discovered after opening-up is documented and priced for approval before additional work.

## Rough estimate examples
For a 1,200 sq. ft. carpet-area home, the indicative Premium interior calculation is 1,200 × ₹1,950 = ₹23,40,000, before excluded taxes and scope. This is arithmetic using the current reference rate, not a quotation or promise. If the user gives built-up area, ask for carpet area or clearly mark any calculation as based on the supplied approximate figure without treating it as a confirmed carpet measurement.

## Lead handoff minimum
Capture a name and one reachable contact route, city, project type, approximate carpet area, budget (or “not decided”), desired start window, and consent to be contacted. Never collect government identity numbers, payment card data, or sensitive credentials. A lead can be saved with fields still unknown, but tell the user what one or two details would help the team.
